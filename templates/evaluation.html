Interview & Placement Talking Points

  • Zero-Phase Filtering: Forward-backward Butterworth filtering prevents phase distortion and timing shift of R-peaks.
  • Pan-Tompkins Algorithm: Differentiation, squaring, and integration isolate steep QRS slopes while refractory blanking prevents false triggers from high T-waves.
  • Isolation Forest Anomaly Scoring: Unsupervised decision trees isolate ectopic morphologies in fewer partitions without requiring manual annotations.
