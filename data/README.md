# MIT-BIH Arrhythmia Database

The MIT-BIH Arrhythmia Database contains 48 half-hour excerpts of two-channel ambulatory ECG recordings, obtained from 47 subjects studied by the BIH Arrhythmia Laboratory. The recordings were digitized at 360 samples per second per channel with 11-bit resolution over a 10 mV range.

Reference: [PhysioNet MIT-BIH Arrhythmia Database](https://physionet.org/content/mitdb/1.0.0/)

## Pre-cached Records
- **Record 100**: Baseline normal sinus rhythm with very few isolated ectopics.
- **Record 106**: Frequent premature ventricular contractions (PVCs).
- **Record 119**: Ventricular bigeminy (alternating normal and ectopic beats).
- **Record 200**: Complex ventricular arrhythmias and couplets.

Processed files and compressed fast cache records are located in `data/cache_npz/`.
